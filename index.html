AGENT DESK v1.8 ledger: starting

6 agents per coin · live prices · paper trades only

TOTAL $300.00 00:00 UTC

Prices, volume, open interest, funding and order book come live from public exchange APIs (Bybit first, then Binance, OKX and KuCoin as backup, USDT perpetuals). Each desk has its own $150 paper bank. Trades are paper only: filled at the last price, 5x of the bank, 0.055% taker fee per side, take profit +0.40%, stop −0.20%, or exit after 15 minutes. After each trade the desk waits 10 minutes. v1.0: when the formula wants a trade, a Claude model must agree first (it sees the market snapshot and recent trades). v1.1: each coin also runs a limit-order twin on the same signals (maker fee 0.02%, rests at the near side of the book for up to 3 minutes, may miss the fill) so market and limit results can be compared. v1.8: the fade bot also asks Claude every 20 seconds whether to close early (exit judge). v1.7: a Fade bot on each coin copies the manual range pattern (buy dips, short bounces) on its own $150 bank. v1.6: manual TP and SL reset to blank after every close or cancel, and a blank box means 3% from entry. v1.5: TP and SL of a manual order or open position can be changed with UPDATE TP / SL. v1.4: signals, Claude's verdicts, orders, fills, misses and cancels are logged to Supabase (desk_events). v1.3: manual order TP and SL are now prices. v1.2: a Manual order panel on each coin lets you place paper market or limit orders yourself, with your own TP and SL (own $150 bank, not part of the agent comparison). The formula only proposes on on a 6-minute move of at least 0.15% (BTC) or 0.30% (NEAR). Not financial advice.
